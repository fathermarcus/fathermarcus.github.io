November 29 – December 24, 2026

A traditional Advent

Before the lights and the shopping, the Church kept Advent as a season of watching and longing. Bring it back to your table this year.

Rorate caeli desuper, et nubes pluant iustum.

“Drop down dew, ye heavens, from above, and let the clouds rain the Just One.” (Isaiah 45:8)

The Advent wreath

Put a simple wreath on your table with four candles: three violet and one rose. Light one more each Sunday, and pray at supper.

Collect of the First Sunday of Advent

Excita, quaesumus, Domine, potentiam tuam, et veni: ut ab imminentibus peccatorum nostrorum periculis, te mereamur protegente eripi, te liberante salvari.

Stir up Thy might, we beseech Thee, O Lord, and come: that from the threatening dangers of our sins we may deserve to be rescued by Thy protection, and saved by Thy deliverance.

At each lighting you can simply pray together: “Come, Lord Jesus.” Then one Our Father, one Hail Mary and one Glory Be.

The great “O” Antiphons

From December 17 the Church sings one ancient antiphon each evening, calling on Christ by His titles from the prophets. Read the one for today after supper. (English: our translation.)
