PRAYER SOURCES · Pray With Father Marcus (fathermarcus.github.io)
Texts used in prayers.js (prayers page and home page), advent.html (Collect, Rorate, O Antiphons).
Latin texts are identical in all languages. English texts are the original site texts (unchanged).
STATUS: verified = checked word for word against the source · adapted = small change, explained · OUR TRANSLATION / NOT VERIFIED = to be checked by a priest or with a printed book.
Changes to the sources: only capitalisation of pronouns (Tu/Vous/Du), 'Amen' added where missing, typos fixed.

CONSECRATION CARD (card.js)
======================================================================
The short consecration prayers on the card are our own devotional texts (no source). The English originals were translated
faithfully into es/fr/de. The 4 new prayers (Immaculate Conception, Guadalupe, Lourdes, Altötting) were written in the same style:
no indulgences or promises. Quoted words of Our Lady to St. Juan Diego ("¿No estoy yo aquí, que soy tu Madre?") come from the Nican Mopohua.
Invocations: traditional ejaculations (e.g. "O Mary, conceived without sin, pray for us who have recourse to thee", Miraculous Medal).
Latin card lines: Cor Iesu sacratissimum, miserere nobis · Cor Mariae immaculatum, ora pro nobis · O Maria, sine labe concepta, ora pro nobis qui ad te confugimus ·
Sancta Dei Genetrix, ora pro nobis · Sancta Maria de Guadalupe, ora pro nobis · Domina nostra de Lourdes, ora pro nobis (simple Latin invocations, not liturgical texts).
