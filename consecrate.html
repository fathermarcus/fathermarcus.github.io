How to pray it at home

A short family ceremony

🕯️
GatherAt the door or before a crucifix. Light a candle if you can.
✝️
PrayThe Sign of the Cross, one Our Father, one Hail Mary, one Glory Be.
📖
ReadA parent reads the prayer on the card aloud. Everyone answers “Amen.”
🏠
Hang itWhere all can see it, and say the invocation together each night.

This card is a devotional aid, not a liturgical rite. If you can, ask your parish priest to bless your home.
