Consecrate your home

For centuries Catholic families have placed their homes under the protection of the Sacred Heart, of Our Lady, of St. Joseph and of St. Michael. Make a card with your family's name, frame it by your door, and pray it together.

Your names never leave your device. Nothing is sent or stored.

Then share it with your family on WhatsApp, and pray the prayer on the card together this Sunday.

How to pray it at home

A short family ceremony

  1. 1. Gather the family at the door or before a crucifix. Light a candle if you can.
  2. 2. Make the Sign of the Cross and pray one Our Father, one Hail Mary and one Glory Be.
  3. 3. The father or mother of the family reads the prayer on the card aloud; everyone answers “Amen.”
  4. 4. Hang the card where everyone will see it, and pray the short invocation at the bottom each night.

This card is a devotional aid, not a liturgical rite. If you can, ask your parish priest to bless your home: it is a beautiful sacramental of the Church.
